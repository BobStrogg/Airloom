# Carrot Cake

A deeply spiced, impossibly moist carrot cake with browned butter cream cheese frosting and salted caramel drizzle.

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cardamom
- Pinch of allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup vegetable oil
- 3/4 cup unsalted butter, browned and cooled
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup dark brown sugar, packed
- 4 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Zest of 1 orange
- 3 cups finely grated carrots (about 5-6 medium carrots)
- 1/2 cup well-drained crushed pineapple
- 1 cup walnuts or pecans, toasted and chopped
- 1/2 cup sweetened shredded coconut
- 1/2 cup raisins (optional)

### Browned Butter Cream Cheese Frosting

- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, browned and cooled to room temperature
- 3 cups powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Zest of 1 orange or lemon
- Pinch of salt

### Salted Caramel Drizzle

- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 teaspoon flaky sea salt

## Instructions

### Brown the Butter (for cake and frosting)

1. Cut **1 1/4 cups (2.5 sticks)** of butter into pieces and place in a light-coloured saucepan over medium heat.
2. Swirl occasionally as the butter melts, foams, and the milk solids turn golden brown with a nutty aroma — about **5-7 minutes**.
3. Immediately pour into a heatproof bowl to stop cooking. Measure out **3/4 cup** for the cake and reserve the rest for the frosting.
4. Let both portions cool completely (refrigerate the frosting portion until it returns to a soft, spreadable consistency).

### Toast the Nuts

1. Spread walnuts or pecans on a baking sheet and toast at **350°F (175°C)** for **8-10 minutes**, stirring halfway, until fragrant and golden.
2. Let cool, then roughly chop. Set aside a handful of whole halves for garnish.

### Cake

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)**. Grease and flour three 8-inch round cake pans.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cardamom, allspice, and salt.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together the vegetable oil, browned butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, eggs, vanilla, and orange zest until well combined.
4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, stirring until just combined.
5. Fold in the grated carrots, crushed pineapple, toasted nuts, coconut, and raisins if using.
6. Divide the batter evenly among the three prepared pans.
7. Bake for **25-30 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8. Let the cakes cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks to cool completely.

### Frosting

1. Beat the cream cheese and cooled browned butter together until smooth and fluffy.
2. Add the vanilla extract, citrus zest, and salt. Mix to combine.
3. Gradually add the powdered sugar, beating on low speed until incorporated, then increase speed and beat until smooth and creamy.

### Salted Caramel Drizzle

1. Heat the sugar in a heavy saucepan over medium heat, swirling (not stirring) until it melts into an amber caramel.
2. Remove from heat and carefully whisk in the butter (it will bubble vigorously).
3. Slowly pour in the heavy cream, whisking until smooth. Stir in the flaky sea salt.
4. Let cool until thickened but still pourable.

### Assembly

1. Place one cake layer on a serving plate. Spread a generous layer of frosting on top.
2. Add the second layer, frost, then place the third layer on top.
3. Frost the top and sides of the cake with the remaining frosting.
4. Drizzle the salted caramel over the top, letting it run down the sides.
5. Garnish with toasted nut halves, candied carrot curls, or a light dusting of cinnamon.

## Tips

- Finely grate the carrots for the best texture. Avoid large shreds.
- Bring the cream cheese to room temperature before making the frosting to avoid lumps.
- Drain the crushed pineapple thoroughly — press it in a fine mesh strainer to remove excess liquid.
- The browned butter should be cooled but still liquid when added to the batter; for the frosting, it should be cooled to a soft, spreadable consistency.
- The cake can be stored in the refrigerator for up to **5 days**. Bring to room temperature for 30 minutes before serving for the best flavour.

## Servings

12-16 slices
